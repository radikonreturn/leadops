# LeadOps design system

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Direction

An operations ledger for a focused B2B sales desk: warm stone workspace surfaces, an ink-blue navigation rail, measured emerald status signals, and compact data-first composition.

## Typography

Geist Sans is used with firm weight contrast, compact table text, and tabular numerals for metrics and dates.

## Color

Ink navy anchors navigation and high-emphasis actions. Off-white and stone define the working surface. Emerald, amber, blue, red, and slate communicate lead states without decorative color.

## Interaction

Controls have clear hover and focus states. A right-side detail drawer retains the lead list context; inline status selection commits directly to the API. Empty, loading, and API-error states explain recovery.
